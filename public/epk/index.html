DJ / Music Selector — Koh Samui

Biography

DJ BUBA has been performing professionally since 2020, with island residencies on Koh Samui since 2022. A bass player since the age of 15, his musical background gives him a strong foundation in rhythm, groove, phrasing, and musicality.

His sets are built around reading the room and understanding how energy should develop throughout the night. Rather than following a fixed formula, BUBA adapts his sound, tempo, and intensity to suit the venue, the moment, and the crowd.

His sound moves through Afro House, Organic House, House, and Indie Dance — combining warm grooves, organic textures, and driving rhythms to create sets that feel musical, dynamic, and naturally connected to the atmosphere of the space.
